# Pearlrow

A four-in-a-row game in one HTML file, built by a single prompt in Cursor (Grok 4.7, medium effort).

Play it: https://gradedprompts.github.io/pearlrow/

`index.html` is the unedited output of one run of the prompt. Nothing was changed by hand after the run.
SHA-256: `24891a844d9d724ed4fd3ed1eb11298bc673833a944c74a5e0eddb49513b7795`

How it plays: coral and pearl discs drop into a sea-green frame of 7 columns and 6 rows; line up four across, up or on a diagonal to win. Play the computer at Easy, Normal or Hard, or two players taking turns on one screen. Rematch swaps who moves first, and your wins at each level are saved in your browser.

Click a column to drop a disc, or aim with the arrow keys and drop with Space; the keys 1 to 7 drop straight into a column. On a phone, tap a column, or press, slide to aim and lift to drop. P or Esc pauses, M toggles sound. It needs no network at all. Add `?mode=hard` or `?mode=duo` to the address to start a game straight away.

The prompt that built it is being prepared for Graded Prompts: https://www.gradedprompts.com/prompts/apps
